Unreal Engine 5 Lighting Course

A visual technical-learning project that combines plain-language explanations, presentation design, and short software demonstrations to make Unreal Engine lighting concepts easier to follow.

Overview

Explain the concept. Show the software. Connect the two.

The course was designed around a simple instructional pattern: introduce the idea in writing, explain when it matters, then reinforce it with a visual or recorded Unreal Engine example.

Learning Goal

Make lighting terminology approachable

Technical concepts are translated into direct language so a beginner can understand what the lighting method does before working in the editor.

Teaching Method

Written explanation + visual example

Slides establish the concept and use case, then supporting visuals connect the explanation to what the learner sees in Unreal Engine.

Media

Slides + short demonstrations

The learning material combines designed presentation pages with short recorded software walkthroughs for practical reinforcement.

Course Samples

From definition to hands-on demonstration.

These selected examples move from explaining lighting types, to comparing when each approach makes sense, and finally to a short Unreal Engine demonstration.

Concept introduction: distinguishes built lighting from dynamic lighting and explains the performance idea behind each.
Practical comparison: gives learners examples of when a static or moving lighting setup is the better fit.
Directional Light demonstration: the final media example is the actual recorded Unreal Engine walkthrough.
