Unreal Engine
Game Workshop 2

A university game-development workshop built to make beginner Unreal Engine concepts easier to understand, follow, and teach. I created two versions of the same core curriculum: one for live classroom instruction and one for students working independently.

Overview

Designed for clarity from the start.

The goal was not just to explain Unreal Engine, but to reduce the points where a beginner could get lost. Written explanations were paired with screenshots and visual references throughout both versions of the course.

My Role

Curriculum + instructional design

I created the lesson structure, written explanations, screenshots, visual materials, and supporting training used to help the instructor deliver the curriculum independently.

Audience

Beginner game-development learners

The material introduces Unreal Engine concepts in a way that gives learners both the explanation and the on-screen reference they need to follow along.

Approach

Explain it, then show it

Technical terms are broken into plain-language explanations and reinforced with interface screenshots, examples, and visual callouts.

Delivery Design

One curriculum, two learning formats.

Both versions are visual. The distinction is how the learner is expected to use them: one supports an instructor teaching the room, while the other supports a student working through the material on their own.
